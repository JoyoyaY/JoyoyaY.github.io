1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Guangzhou Qiaoyayang Information Technology Co., Ltd. (“we,” “us,” or “the Company”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our games and related services. These include mobile games, HTML5 and web games distributed through VK Mini Apps and the Transsion gaming platform, and our official website. By using a service, you acknowledge this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

The information involved depends on the game and features you use.

  • Information you provide: account identifiers, nickname, and avatar authorized by platforms such as VK or RuStore; details and attachments you send when contacting support; and information you submit in surveys or activities.
  • Information collected automatically: device model, operating system, device identifiers (such as Android ID, OAID, or IDFA), screen resolution, language, time zone, network type, IP address, usage and game progress logs, login times, crash reports, and error data.
  • Platform information: when you use our games through VK, RuStore, or Transsion, we may receive information necessary to provide the service, such as an account identifier, payment receipt, or order status, subject to that platform’s authorization process and policy.

We do not actively collect government ID numbers, biometric data, precise location, contacts, text messages, or photo library contents. If a feature requires sensitive information, we will request your explicit consent beforehand.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain game features, save progress, support interactions, and process purchase orders.
  • To understand usage and improve gameplay, level balance, design, and user experience.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, cheating, malicious software, attacks, and other security risks.
  • To respond to inquiries, complaints, and feedback and provide technical support.
  • To comply with applicable laws and lawful requests from authorities.
  • With your consent where required, to recommend relevant games, content, or events.

We will seek your consent before using personal information for a purpose not described here.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary information with platform partners to operate games and process payments, and with service providers supporting analytics, payment processing, cloud storage, or customer support. These providers must protect information and process it according to our instructions and applicable requirements. We may also disclose information when required by law or a competent authority, or as part of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or asset transfer. In a business transfer, the recipient will remain bound by this Policy or seek consent where required.

5. Storage and Security

Personal information collected and generated in mainland China is stored in mainland China. We retain information only for as long as needed for the purposes described here, then delete or anonymize it unless law requires otherwise. We use measures such as TLS/SSL encryption, access controls, permissions, and audit logs, and provide security training. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will notify you as required by law. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure; please protect your account and device.

6. Third-Party Services and SDKs

Our games may use third-party services or SDKs for analytics, crash and performance monitoring, advertising, payments, and platform login (including VK and RuStore). These providers have their own privacy policies. We assess SDK security and require appropriate handling of information; please review the relevant provider’s policy when using its features.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and HTML5 games may use cookies, LocalStorage, and web beacons to remember preferences and login status, measure usage, improve services, and protect accounts and transactions. You can manage or clear cookies in your browser. Disabling them may affect some features.

8. Children

We take children’s privacy seriously. If you are under 18, read this Policy with a parent or guardian and use our services with their consent. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information without prior guardian consent. If we learn that we have done so, we will delete it as soon as practicable. A guardian may contact us using the details below.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction or deletion of your personal information, withdraw consent, close your game account, or lodge a complaint with a regulator. To exercise these rights, contact us below. After verifying your identity, we generally respond within 15 business days.

10. International Transfers

Because our games are distributed in Russia and other markets worldwide, information may be transferred between countries or regions to provide the services. We follow applicable cross-border transfer requirements and use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or security assessments where required.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy as our business, laws, or technology change. The revised version will appear on this page with an updated date. For material changes, such as a substantial change in collection purposes or sharing, we will provide prominent notice and obtain renewed consent where required. Please review this Policy periodically.
